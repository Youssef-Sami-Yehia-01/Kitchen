## Corrections in this review

- R06 is a coffee table, with its previous centre retained. R05 remains the sofa.
- R03 has a wall-mounted reflective mirror, not a picture.
- All three bathroom mirrors and the dressing mirror attach to the actual backing wall. Mirror planes are checked after world-coordinate transforms.
- The guest basin faces into the room. Assumed bathroom 1 fixtures meet their backing walls.
- Both showers place the fittings on the north/shaft-side solid wall. The fixed glass and hinged entry door are on the room side. Shower doors open on the side away from the toilet.
- Seat/counter heights, loose cushions, tabletop accessories and the main-bedroom curtain mounting side were corrected.
- There are no indoor floor planters. Indoor plants sit on existing counters, consoles, shelves or tables. The four balcony pots and the low trough clear furniture, columns and door swings. Right-balcony pots leave over 900 mm of clear floor width.
- R05 architectural dimensions and all owner-recorded appliance widths/depths are retained. These are 3D review corrections; the earlier issued CAD file has not been overwritten.
